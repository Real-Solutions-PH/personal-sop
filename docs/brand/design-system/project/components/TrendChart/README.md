# TrendChart

A metric over time with its average, goal and projection, readable without a tooltip and explorable with one.

Daily values as small dots at 35%, the 7-day average as a 2 pt line ending on a dot, the goal as a dashed `good` line, three quiet gridlines labelled on the right in mono, and the first and last dates. Above: today's value, the rate of change as a word ("−0.3 kg / week"), and the goal. Below: a legend and one sentence of projection ("you reach 72.0 kg around 5 Nov"). Touch or hover shows a crosshair and a readout of that day.
