# Logo

The Unfurl mark is the **fiddlehead**: one stroke rising from a stem into a spiral, with two young leaflets. A fern frond opens from that spiral a little at a time, which is the product's promise. The mark is Fern's face as well as the app's, so the logo, the app icon and the companion are one object.

**Lockup:** the fiddlehead followed by the lowercase wordmark "unfurl" in Geist Medium, tracking −3%; the mark in `primary` (moss), the word in `foreground`. Mark height ≈ 1.1 × the wordmark size, gap 0.16 em.

**The logo is always flat.** No gradients, glows or shadows on the mark, the lockup or the app icon: one solid colour behind one solid line. That keeps it modern and calm at every size.

**App icon:** the white fiddlehead on a solid moss (`#1f5a43`) square, at about 66% of the width. iOS dark icon: the mark in `sprout` on `#121513`. Tinted and Android themed icons: the mark alone as a single-colour layer.

**Sizes and stroke:** 24-unit grid, stroke 2 units at 28 pt and above, 2.4 below; round caps and joins. Smallest mark 16 pt; below that use the orb without the mark. Clear space: half the mark's width on every side.

**Don't:** put it on a gradient, outline it, add shadows or glows, recolour it outside moss, white, `sprout` or `foreground`, rotate it at rest, or set the wordmark in caps or another typeface.

Inside the app, the same mark becomes Fern: there, and only there, it gains the orb gradient and glow, so the companion feels alive while the logo stays flat.
