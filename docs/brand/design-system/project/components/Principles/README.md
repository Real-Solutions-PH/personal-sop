# Principles

How the voice (calm, plain, specific) becomes visual rules, plus the two rules that make the product: **insight at a glance** and **Fern first**.

**Insight at a glance**
- Getting information out is as effortless as putting it in. Every number shows its unit, its change and its shape; charts print their values.
- Every metric shows three things: the number (with unit), the change (with what it is compared to) and the shape (a sparkline or chart).
- Every number can be tapped to see its chart and asked about in Fern.

**Calm chrome, colourful data**
- The frame is quiet: paper `background`, white `card`, ink `foreground`, hairlines, one moss button.
- Colour lives in the data: the area colours (`tsk`, `fit`, `fin`, `hab`, `life`, `nut`) fill charts, sparklines, heatmaps and progress, and are validated as a chart palette (colour-blind safe, 3:1 on card, both themes). Text stays in ink.
- Fern is the only gradient, glow and motion.

**Plain**
- Flat cards with a 1 pt `border`; shadows only on things that float.
- Sentence case, no exclamation marks, no emoji. One filled button per screen.

**Specific**
- Numbers in Geist Mono with their unit, and changes with their baseline: "+12% vs last wk", "−0.3 kg / week".
- Every record Fern makes shows the fields it filled and the words it came from.

**Fern first**
- Fern is how people log and how they ask. The Fern button rises out of the tab bar on every screen and opens Fern's drawer.
